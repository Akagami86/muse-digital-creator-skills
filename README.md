MUSE DIGITAL CREATOR SKILLS

An AI-powered digital creative production system.

Muse Digital Creator Skills adalah kumpulan skill untuk membantu AI Agent merencanakan, mengembangkan, memproduksi, dan mengevaluasi berbagai kebutuhan kreatif digital secara terstruktur.

Sistem ini dirancang dengan pembagian peran, alur kerja bertahap, strategi konsep, dan pengawasan kualitas sebelum hasil akhir diserahkan.

Core Architecture

Sistem ini memiliki lima peran utama:

Agent| Responsibility
Dayita| Orchestrator, task routing, execution, and delivery
Hermes| Research, audience analysis, market research, and references
Strategist| Creative concept development, evaluation, and concept selection
Creative Studio| Creative production through specialized skills
Supervisor| Independent quality control and approval decisions

Creative Specialist Skills

Direktori "digital-creator-system" berisi master workflow dan spesialisasi berikut:

1. AI Image Creator — Image-generation briefs and visual prompts.
2. Brand Identity — Brand positioning and visual identity.
3. Canva Designer — Canva-ready layouts and graphic design.
4. Copywriting Marketing — Marketing copy and persuasive content.
5. Creative Strategist — Creative concepts and strategic selection.
6. Creative Supervisor — Independent review and quality control.
7. Digital Product Creator — Ebooks, templates, workbooks, and digital products.
8. UI/UX Designer — User flows, wireframes, and interface design.
9. Video Content Creator — Video concepts, scripts, and storyboards.

Mandatory Workflow

Every project follows this controlled pipeline:

1. Brief & Intake — Dayita identifies objectives, audience, requirements, and deliverables.
2. Research — Hermes gathers relevant information and references.
3. Concept Development — Strategist develops 3–7 distinct concepts and selects one winning direction.
4. Concept Approval — Supervisor evaluates the selected concept.
5. Creative Production — Creative Studio executes the approved concept.
6. Final Quality Control — Supervisor evaluates the actual deliverable.
7. Final Delivery — Dayita integrates and delivers the completed work.

Supervisor Decision System

Supervisor acts as an independent internal judge.

- APPROVED — The concept or deliverable meets the required standards.
- CONDITIONAL — Corrections are required before approval.
- REJECTED — The concept or deliverable must be reworked.

Full production must not begin before concept approval.

Strategic Principles

- Generate 3–7 genuinely different creative concepts.
- Evaluate concepts using a weighted scoring system.
- Select and lock one winning concept before production.
- Preserve the approved creative direction throughout production.
- Evaluate final deliverables against the original brief.
- Prioritize originality, usefulness, feasibility, and platform readiness.
- Never fabricate research, results, testimonials, or completed actions.
